About UcFeri

Fostering Innovation and Entrepreneurship Excellence

UcFeri serves as the University of Venda's premier centre for entrepreneurship and innovation, building on the university's 40+ year legacy of academic excellence. We are committed to developing entrepreneurial leaders who will drive economic transformation across Africa.

  • State-of-the-art facilities for research and development
  • Expert guidance from industry leaders and successful entrepreneurs
  • Strong partnerships with government and private sector

Our Programs

Comprehensive Support for Entrepreneurs

Startup Incubation Program

Our comprehensive 12-month incubation program provides startups with workspace, mentorship, and access to funding opportunities.

  • Dedicated workspace and facilities
  • One-on-one mentorship sessions
  • Access to funding networks
  • Legal and business support
Apply Now

Entrepreneurship Education

Practical learning pathways designed for students, graduates, and early-stage founders who want to build sustainable ventures and innovation-led careers.

  • Certificate and diploma entrepreneurship tracks
  • Design thinking, business model, and market validation workshops
  • Financial literacy, bookkeeping, and startup operations modules
  • Pitch communication and investor-readiness training
Explore Courses

Mentorship & Coaching

Personalized support from experienced entrepreneurs, academics, and sector experts to help founders make better strategic, product, and go-to-market decisions.

  • One-on-one mentor matching based on startup stage and sector
  • Monthly coaching clinics focused on growth bottlenecks
  • Founder accountability sessions and milestone tracking
  • Access to industry guest mentors and alumni founders
Find a Mentor

Access to Finance

Funding support for promising ventures through readiness coaching, strategic introductions, and opportunities to connect with grant and investment partners.

  • Grant application guidance and proposal review support
  • Investor pitch deck development and due diligence preparation
  • Demo day access with angel, VC, and development finance networks
  • Financial planning support for scalable growth
